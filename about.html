👤 About

Welcome to the official site of GlayMirror — a 16-year-old underground rapper, writer, and lyrical craftsman. Known for his raw emotions, intense wordplay, and fearless delivery, GM represents a new wave of real hip hop coming straight from the soul. Inspired by legends like Kr$na, Raftaar, Eminem and poetic minds like Jaun Elia, GlayMirror doesn’t just rap — he writes truths that cut deep. Every track is built in his home studio, with honesty as the foundation and legacy as the goal. This isn’t just music — it’s a movement.

Whether it's a freestyle, a concept EP, or a lyrical journey — we aim to break norms, knock on closed doors, and make our mark. Stick around, stream the music, and be part of the legacy. 🔥
